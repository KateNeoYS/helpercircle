Her long-term record

What nearly six years in one house tells you.

Ivy came to Singapore in December 2020 and has worked for one family ever since. Their elder child was two; the younger was five months old. Both of those children are now at school, and she is still there. Nearly six years, one household, no gaps — and no other employer in the country to compare.

What that job has been: the cooking and the running of the house, and the two children from a baby and a toddler through to eight and six. Her permit scope names the care of an infant, a toddler and a child, which is unusual on one record and simply reflects that she has been there long enough for the same children to pass through all three.

The household is a large one. Her first contract was with her ma’am’s family, six people; since then she has been with her sir’s, seven. Three families across two houses, as she describes it, with two helpers between them because the grandfather lives there. She has helped care for both grandparents alongside the children and the house.

One thing to be clear about: she did not sleep with the baby and has never done night duties. If you are hiring for an infant and expect the nights covered, that is not what these six years were.

And the honest limit of this signal: nobody has recommended her to us. Her record is long and clean, but it is one family’s worth of it, and a long stay is not the same thing as a family saying they would have her again.

Where she’d suit next: a family with young children who want someone who has actually stayed — and who will not mind that her experience is deep in one household rather than spread across several. She is used to a busy house, shared work, and grandparents in the mix.

Her employment history is available to interested families, with her consent. This profile carries no personal recommendation from an employer.
