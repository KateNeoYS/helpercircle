Her long-term record

What eight years in Singapore tells you.

Judy has been in Singapore since September 2018, and spent nearly six of those eight years with one family — a Chinese household in a landed home, whose two children grew from teenagers into their twenties while she was there. Six years in one house is not a thing that happens by accident.

She has been with her present family since November 2024, cooking and cleaning. Her employer plans the dinner menu; Judy cooks it. The photographs above are hers.

Between those two placements sits a two-month one, and we would rather explain it than leave you to find it on her record. It was a household with two helpers and the duties split between them. That arrangement did not settle, and she moved on. Everything either side of it is measured in years.

Where she’d thrive next: a settled home that wants the kitchen and the housekeeping in steady hands, and would be glad of proper Chinese home cooking most nights.

Her employment history is available to interested families, with her consent. This profile carries no personal recommendation from a previous employer.
