Her record

Seven years of household work, and a family who renewed her.

2019 to 2021 — two years in Saudi Arabia as a nanny and helper: the children’s day, the housekeeping, the laundry and the meals. This period sits outside Singapore, so it does not appear on her Singapore employment record.

February 2022 to February 2025 — her first Singapore family, Singaporean Chinese, a household of four, and the heart of her record. Her employment record carries this as one unbroken registration across three years, which is what a renewed contract looks like. She took their child from newborn to three years old, alongside the cooking, the cleaning, the laundry and the ironing. The family renewed her contract once, and she stayed three years in all. It ended only because the children had started school and a full-time helper was no longer needed.

February 2025 to now — a Filipino mother and Chinese father with a seven-year-old, the cooking and the house. She is transferring in November after a disagreement with her employer. She is open about it and will talk it through; the family are helping her find a replacement, which tells its own story.

Where she’d suit next. A family with a baby or young children — taking a child from the first weeks through to three is the thing she has done longest and the reason a family kept renewing her. She cooks everyday Chinese food, works to instructions, and has not taken on eldercare or pets.

Her employment history is available to interested families, with her consent.
