Her employer’s recommendation

In Sheila’s words, and ours.

Julie came to Singapore in May 2015 and spent her first six years with one family, taking their children from eleven months and three years old. Since then she has worked for two more households — Sheila’s, from December 2024 to October 2025, and her present family since then.

What Sheila wrote: “She is a mature, easygoing and dependable person who is always willing to help. She never complains and approaches her work with a positive attitude. One of the qualities we value most about Julie is her honesty. We have been able to trust her, particularly when it comes to caring for our children.”

The nights. Sheila’s youngest was eleven months old when Julie took him on, and she did his night feeds as well as his days. “She never complained about having to wake up for night feeds. Her willingness to do this without complaint showed us how committed and caring she is.” A family with a baby will know how rare that is.

She is used to sharing a house. Sheila’s household had a second helper, and so does her present one. “She gets along very well with our other helper and is easy to work with. She is respectful, cooperative and has a calm and mature personality.”

Why she is available, in her own words: no negative reason. She needs a family arranged before she flies home on 27 September, so that she can return to Singapore on 3 November. Her present employer understood she might not be coming back after home leave, and has given her a letter explaining it.

Where she’d suit next: a family with a baby or small children, particularly one where the nights are the hard part. She has done six years in one house, and the employer who had her most recently asked her more than once to stay.

Sheila has offered to answer questions from interested families directly.
