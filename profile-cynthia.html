Her record

Two years in Singapore, and a clear idea of what she wants next.

2019 to 2020 — retail in the Philippines. Two years on a shop floor, serving customers and keeping the displays and the day running.

2023 to 2024 — caregiving in the Philippines. A year looking after someone recovering from a stroke: the daily routine, personal care, meals, a clean and safe room, and company.

December 2024 to now — two years. Her first job in Singapore and her only one: a couple in a private flat and their baby, with the cooking, the cleaning, the ironing and the marketing. She learned to cook her employers’ food properly — the Indian dishes below are hers. Her contract ends in December and she is not recontracting.

What she wants next, in her own words. Older children rather than a baby, a household without pets, and not eldercare — she has done it, and would rather not take it on again for now. A family who wants those things will find her a straightforward fit; one who doesn’t is better knowing now.

Where she’d suit next. A family with school-age children who want the house run and their own food cooked properly — particularly an Indian or vegetarian household, since that is the kitchen she has spent two years in. She is leaving a small flat and a quiet job, and is looking for more to do.
