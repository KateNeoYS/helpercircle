Her record in Singapore

Nine years, four families — and one six-week job that explains the rest.

Richiel has been a helper for twelve years — two in Saudi Arabia, and nine in Singapore since September 2017. Her record has one very short placement in it, and rather than leave a family to find it, here is what happened.

September 2017 to August 2021 — a Singaporean family of five, with children of one, four and seven. Nearly four years: the three children, the cleaning, the marketing and the car.

August 2021 to May 2023 — a Sri Lankan family and their baby of eight months. This is where she learned to cook Sri Lankan. It ended early, on their side rather than hers: their arrangements changed and they no longer needed a helper full time.

May to June 2023 — six weeks. A New Zealand family in a three-storey landed house with a pool, two children and a dog. In her own words: “I can’t handle everything.” She said so and she left. It is the shortest job on her record and she does not dress it up — and her employment record confirms the detail, right down to the dog.

June 2023 to October 2024 — back with the Sri Lankan family. When they heard she had transferred, they called and asked her to return. She went back, and stayed another sixteen months. A family choosing to re-employ someone they had already let go is a stronger statement than most written references. It ended when their daughter lost her job and they could no longer keep a helper.

October 2024 to now — a French family of four in a five-room flat. Their newborn and a four-year-old, the cooking, the cleaning and the marketing. Two years, and the contract runs to October 2026 — but the family no longer need a helper and will release her sooner.

Where she’d suit next: a family with a baby or small children in a flat or condominium, who want the cooking done as well as the children. She has asked for no dog and no elderly care, and after the six weeks in 2023 that is worth taking at face value rather than testing.

Her employment history is available to interested families, with her consent. This profile carries no personal recommendation from an employer.
