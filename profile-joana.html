One family, three children, and nearly seven years.

January 2020 to now — six years and eight months. A household of five in a condominium: a Singaporean Chinese father, a Filipino mother and three children. The cooking, the house, and the children from the start.

She arrived when the eldest was one year old. The twins were born after she joined, and she has had them from their first weeks. All three are still in her care today — a seven-year-old and six-year-old twins. Taking two babies at once, in a house that already had a toddler, is the part of this record worth pausing over.

Nothing else is on her record. One employer, one unbroken registration, no transfers and no gaps since she arrived in Singapore. That family renewed her contract more than once.

Why she is leaving. She would like a better salary after nearly seven years at the same rate, and a different household to work in. She says so plainly, and we would rather pass it on plainly than dress it up as something else.

Where she’d suit next. A family with young children, and particularly one expecting a baby or with more than one small child at once — she has done exactly that, from birth, for six years. She is used to a condominium household with both parents at home.

Her full employment history is available to interested families, with her consent.
