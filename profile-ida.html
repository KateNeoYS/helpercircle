Her record

One family, ten years, and nothing else on the page.

August 2016 to now — ten years and two months. A household of four in a five-room flat: the cooking, the laundry, the house, and the family’s children, who were at primary school when she arrived and are grown now. It is the only job she has held in Singapore.

There is nothing else to report, and that is the point. One employer, one unbroken registration, no transfers and no gaps in ten years. A family who keep someone that long have renewed her contract four times over, and a helper who stays that long has made a home of the arrangement. It is the longest single placement on this site.

Why she is leaving. The family are returning to India. That is the whole reason, and they are providing a release letter on her way out.

What she can and cannot do. She cooks Western food, Malay dishes and simple Chinese, and she bakes and fries — the doughnuts below are hers. She has not cared for a baby or a toddler, has no experience with pets, and has not done eldercare. Her employer’s mother visited from time to time, which is not the same thing, and we would rather say so than let it be assumed.

Where she’d suit next. A household with school-age children or none at all, who want the cooking and the running of the house in steady hands. Ten years in one home says more about how she works than any list could — she settles, and she stays.

Her full employment history is available to interested families, with her consent.
