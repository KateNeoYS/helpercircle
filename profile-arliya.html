The essentials

Currently

Nine years with an Indian family. She took on their newborn and a six-year-old, and has been with the household ever since, including some night waking in the early years.

Cooking

Indian and Mexican dishes for her present family, and Chinese and Western from her earlier households. Her employer describes her preparing a wide variety of meals.

With the children

Not only minding them. She sits with them and teaches them to read. Her employer’s words are patience, care and responsibility.

Before this family

From 2015 to 2017 with a household of three adults and a three-year-old grandson. Before that, Singaporean Chinese households from 2008.

Housekeeping

Her employer calls her capable and thorough, and says she takes pride in keeping the home clean, organised and well maintained.

Eighteen years in Singapore

Working here since 2008, with the last nine of them in a single household.

Why she is moving on

After nine years the children have grown up and the family no longer need a live-in helper. Her employer wrote her reference themselves.

Her employer’s recommendation

In her employer’s words, and ours.

Arliya has been with this family since 2017. She joined when their younger child was a newborn and the elder was six, and she is still with them now.

In her employer’s own words:

“Arliya has been our domestic helper and a valued member of our household for close to nine years. During this time, Arliya has consistently been responsible, hardworking, trustworthy, and dependable. She has played an important role in looking after our home and family.”

“Arliya is a good cook and can cook Indian as well Western dishes very well. She prepares a wide variety of dishes and has always taken care to prepare meals that our family enjoys.”

“She is also very capable and thorough when it comes to housekeeping and cleaning. She takes pride in keeping the home clean, organised, and well maintained.”

“Arliya has been very good with childcare, showing patience, care, and responsibility when looking after our children.”

“I am confident that she will bring the same level of reliability, hard work, cooking skills, housekeeping ability, and genuine care to her next household.”

Where she’d thrive next: a family who want the cooking taken seriously and children looked after properly, in a home she can settle into. Nine years in one household, on top of the years before it, says she stays where she is happy.

Her employer’s letter is available to interested families, and she has offered to answer questions directly.
