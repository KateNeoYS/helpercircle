Available from

30 November 2026. Transfer within Singapore.

Why she is available

The mother is changing jobs and the baby is going into infant care, so the household will not need a helper. They wrote her reference as she leaves.

Where she is now

Since July 2026 — three children: six, three and a baby of two months. Her record carries it as cooking, general household and the care of an infant, toddler and child.

Nearly six years, one household

July 2009 to June 2015 — her first Singapore household, on one unbroken registration. The longest thing on her record.

Children she has cared for

A baby of two months now, with a three- and a six-year-old. An infant and toddler from 2022 to 2023, and a toddler and a child for the three years after that.

Thai, Chinese, Western and Japanese. Her employer calls it one of her greatest strengths. There are photographs further down this page.

Pets

Notes from her employer

From Roger’s signed letter, written in November 2026 as Gretchen leaves.

He opens plainly: Gretchen “has been a wonderful help to our family throughout her employment with us”.

On the children: “She has been especially good with our young children, showing genuine care, patience and responsibility in looking after them. We have always felt comfortable entrusting our children to her care, and she has built a lovely relationship with them.”

On the house: “Beyond childcare, she is hardworking, reliable, and capable of managing household chores independently, keeping our home clean, tidy and well-organised.”

On the cooking: “One of her greatest strengths is her cooking. She is an excellent cook who can prepare a wide variety of delicious meals and adapt to our family’s tastes and preferences. Her ability to manage both the children and household responsibilities has made our daily lives considerably easier.”

And at the end: “We are truly grateful for her dedication, positive attitude, and the care she has shown our family. We would gladly recommend her to any family looking for a trustworthy, capable and caring helper.”

Her record

In Singapore since July 2009.

July 2009 to June 2015 — five years and ten months. Her first Singapore household, a family of four in a five-room flat. One unbroken registration, and the longest placement on her record.

June 2015 to April 2016. Three short placements in succession — six months, three weeks, then four months. They sit together in the middle of an otherwise settled record.

April 2016 to March 2019. Two households, fifteen months and then twenty months.

March 2019 to February 2021 — nearly two years. A household of three. It ended when her employer lost his job.

February 2021 to January 2022. Two people in a four-room flat, registered for cooking, general household and the care of an elderly person. It ended when the person she cared for passed away.

January 2022 to August 2023. A household of four in a five-room flat, registered for the care of an infant and a toddler. The family relocated to India.

August 2023 to July 2026 — three years. A household of four, registered for cooking, general household and the care of a toddler and a child.

July 2026 to now. Three children — six, three and a baby of two months — with the cooking and the household besides. It is ending because the mother is changing jobs and the baby is going into infant care.

Where she’d suit next. A family with young children, a baby among them if needed — she has a two-month-old in her care at the moment, and infant care on her record twice over. She cooks Thai, Chinese, Western and Japanese, she has a year of elderly care behind her, and she is comfortable with pets in the house.

Her employment record, her written reference and her full work history are available to interested families, with her consent.
