— Sharifah, her employer from 2020 to 2024

The essentials

Available from

11 December 2026. Transfer within Singapore.

Why she is available

The family she is with now are moving to Germany for work.

Four years, premature twins

April 2020 to March 2024 — a household of six with four children, the twins the youngest. She had them from the day they came home from hospital until they turned four.

Where she is now

Since October 2024 — an Italian family with one daughter, whom she has looked after since she was born. Cooking, general household and the care of an infant and toddler on her record.

Children she has cared for

Babies from newborn up to children of seven. Twins together as toddlers, four children in one household, and a single child in each of the others.

Cooking

Her employer put it plainly: not a gourmet cook, but basic, healthy meals that young children will eat, and she follows a recipe or a family’s instructions closely.

Nearly nine years in Singapore

Since February 2018, across five households — two of them long, two of them short.

Best fit

A family with a baby or young children who will let her get on with the day. She is used to taking children to appointments, classes and the playground on her own.

Notes from her employer

From Sharifah’s signed letter, written at the end of her four years with the twins.

She begins with the job: “She was an integral part of our family for four years, caring for my twin boys from the day they came home from the hospital until they turned four years old.”

On the work itself: “She is a master of multitasking, effortlessly managing the chaotic schedule of twin toddlers. She kept the boys on a consistent routine for meals, naps and playtime, which was crucial for their development. She was also incredibly helpful with sleep training and navigating the various stages of their growth.”

On knowing her way around: “She knows how to navigate public transport with children, is adept at attending medical appointments, and is familiar with the location and logistics of various playgrounds, libraries and enrichment classes. We could entrust her with tasks like bringing the boys to their check-ups or taking them to a class, knowing she would handle everything with complete confidence and competence.”

She is straight about the cooking: “She is not a gourmet cook, but she prepares basic, healthy and appealing meals for young children with care. She is also very capable of following recipes and cooking according to specific instructions.”

And on why they parted: “Our decision to part ways was made reluctantly and solely due to a change in our family’s needs. In her four years with us, we never had to worry about our children’s safety, happiness or well-being when they were in her care.”

Her record

Five households in Singapore since February 2018.

February 2018 to December 2019 — one year and ten months. Her first Singapore household: a Singaporean family of two in a landed property.

December 2019 to April 2020 — three and a half months. An Italian household of three. Her employer was working as a substitute teacher at a school and could not sustain the salary, and asked her to find another employer.

April 2020 to March 2024 — three years and eleven months. A Malaysian-Singaporean household of six: an elder boy, a daughter, and twins born premature whom Girlie took from the day they came home from hospital. There was a second helper for the housework and the cooking while Girlie had the children; that helper went home partway through and Girlie ran both sides on her own. It ended when the family brought that helper back and said the children were old enough now. This is the household that wrote her reference.

March to October 2024 — seven months. A household of three in a private flat. By her own account the hours left her no real break and the work was never counted as finished, however much of it she did. She asked to transfer and the family let her go.

October 2024 to now. An Italian family with one daughter, whom she has looked after since she was born. They are relocating to Germany for work, which is why she is available.

Where she’d suit next. A family with a baby or young children. She has taken two newborns through to toddlerhood and one of those was a pair of premature twins, and she knows the Singapore side of it — the bus and the MRT with a child, the clinic appointments, the classes and the libraries. What she has asked for is a household that trusts her to get on with the day she knows.

Her employment record, her written reference and her full work history are available to interested families, with her consent.
