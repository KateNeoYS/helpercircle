Her record in Singapore

Five households since October 2010, and a career that turned towards care.

Kathy has been in Singapore for sixteen years. What her record shows is someone who started in ordinary household work and moved steadily into care — and then went and got qualified in it.

October 2010 to May 2012 — her first job here, with a newborn. Bottles, bathing, nappies, and sleeping in the baby’s room.

June 2012 to October 2015 — a family of three and their dog. The cleaning, the laundry, the marketing, and a daughter in her last year of primary school. Three years and four months.

December 2016 to November 2020 — a single father and his son, who was in K2. Four years: the child’s routine, the school run, the cooking and the house.

November 2020 to July 2025 — the longest job of her career, and the one that changed it. A household of five in a three-room flat: a grandmother, the parents, their grown son, and a grandfather with dementia. She cared for him through a fall and the decline that followed — walking frame, then stick — and handled the personal care, the meals, the medication reminders and the company. Four years and eight months. She took her Aged Care Provider certificate during it, in 2021, and First Aid and CPR the year after.

July to August 2025 — two months as a health care assistant at Red Crowns Senior Living, a residential care company. Bathing, feeding and mobility for elderly residents, working to care plans alongside nurses. She did this between jobs, of her own accord, to add facility experience to what she had learned in homes.

September 2025 to now — a couple in a four-room flat, and a bedridden stroke patient. This is the most demanding work she has done: bathing, dressing, toileting, feeding, turning to prevent pressure sores, and transfers she manages with the second helper in the house. The two of them work a rota, including overnight.

Where she’d suit next: a family with an elderly parent or a dependent adult at home. She has done dementia over years and stroke care at its heaviest, and she has the certificates to go with the experience. What she has asked for is a household where the rest days and the sleep are real, which is a fair thing to settle before anyone starts.

Her CV, certificates and employment history are available to interested families, with her consent.
