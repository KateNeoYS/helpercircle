The essentials

Available from

9 December 2026, when her contract ends. Her employer will give her a release letter. Transfer within Singapore.

Nearly five years, one household

November 2019 to August 2024 — three adults in a penthouse and their labrador. The dog three times a day, the cooking, the cleaning, the ironing, the car and the marketing.

Her household now

A family of five since December 2024 — three children of one, three and six, and a dog, plus the cooking and the household.

Why she is leaving

The family are moving to a three-storey house with a basement. She says three young children and a house that size is more than she can do properly, so she is finishing the contract rather than carrying on.

Children she has cared for

From one year old upward — a one, a three and a six-year-old now; girls of two and five in her first household; three and seven in another. School runs, meals and the day around them.

Dogs

A labrador walked three times a day for nearly five years, and a dog in her household now. She asks for a home with a pet rather than avoiding one.

Cooking

Chinese home cooking and Western — stir-fries and braises, roasts, steak, tom yum, shepherd’s pie. Photographs below are her own.

What she is looking for

A small family in a condominium, and she is happy for there to be a pet. Her own words, and her record backs them up.

Her record in Singapore

Fifteen years, four households — and one of them for nearly five.

June 2011 to October 2013 — her first Singapore household, a family of four in a five-room flat. Two girls of two and five: the school runs, the housework and the marketing. Two years and three months. She ended it early and went home to deal with a family matter.

August 2016 to June 2017 — a second family of four, two girls of three and seven, with the cooking and the household alongside the school runs. Nine months. This one did not work, and she asked to be released rather than let it drift on. She will explain it plainly if you ask.

November 2019 to August 2024 — four years and ten months with three adults in a penthouse, and their labrador. She walked him three times a day and ran the rest of the house: cooking, cleaning, ironing, the car, the marketing. It ended when her father died and she went home for the funeral. Her employer would not let her return, so she gave up the job. Her employment record shows the ending as her choice, which it was — though not much of one.

December 2024 to December 2026 — a family of five: three children of one, three and six, a dog, and the household. She is seeing the full two-year contract through to its end date, and leaving because the family are moving to a three-storey house with a basement.

Where she’d suit next. A small household in a condominium, with a dog if there is one. She is good with children from about a year old, cooks Chinese and Western, and has twice run a home single-handed including the car. What she has asked not to take on is a large house with several young children at once, and she is saying so before she starts rather than after.

Her full employment history is available to interested families, with her consent.

Photographs Lynivic took of her own cooking — Chinese home dishes and Western family dinners, from braised tofu and basil chicken to steak, a roast chicken platter, tom yum and shepherd’s pie.
