About Jenny

Six and a half years in Singapore, and a family who put their recommendation in writing.

Jenny has been with the same family since December 2023 — nearly three years. Their child — nine when she arrived, twelve now — is her whole responsibility: there is a second helper in the house, and the family cook to their own diet, so the child is where all her attention goes. School, homework, activities and the hours between. They moved from a condominium to a four-room flat during her time with them, and she moved with them. The job ends in November only because they are leaving Singapore for good.

Aira May, her employer, wrote her recommendation unprompted. She describes Jenny as hardworking, responsible, honest and trustworthy — respectful, willing to learn, able to follow instructions and to finish her work without being supervised. “I am happy to recommend Jenny to any prospective employer,” she writes, “and believe she would be a valuable and dependable addition to another household.” She has offered to answer questions from families directly.

Before this family she had a two-year-old and a five-year-old in a landed house for just over two years, and cooking was part of every job she held before this one — Chinese soups, seafood, curry and vegetable fritters, mostly learned from recipes she follows on her own time. The photographs below are her own.

Where she’d suit next. A family with children of school age, or from about two upward. Her strongest work has always been with a child rather than a house — she has not cared for a baby, and would not be the right choice for a newborn. She is comfortable with pets, and has asked not to take on elderly care.

Her employer’s signed letter, and her full employment history, are available to interested families — with her consent.
