The essentials

Available from

Mid-December 2026. Transfer within Singapore.

Where she is now

Since April 2024 — an Australian-Indian family in a condominium, with their daughter, now nine. The cooking, the house, the marketing and the school run.

Babies she has cared for

A newborn girl from birth, for five months, in 2017 — and a baby of six months alongside a three-year-old in 2023. Infant care is on her employment record for that placement.

Training

A cooking course at Expat Kitchen in Singapore and a first-aid course, both in 2017, and a TESDA housekeeping course in the Philippines in 2011.

Cooking

Western and Asian dishes, breakfast, lunch and dinner. Her employer says she follows recipes carefully and looks up new ones herself — which is how she learned to cook for his family.

Pets

Three and a half years with two cats and three dogs in a landed house, and pets on her record again in 2023. No trouble to her.

Expatriate households

British, Australian, Dutch-Brazilian and Australian-Indian — every Singapore family she has worked for, and where she is most at home. Her English is strong.

Why she is available

Her employer’s circumstances have changed and they have agreed to part ways. In his own words, it is in no way a reflection of her work or character.

Notes from her employer

From Ishy’s reference letter, 3 November 2026 — he calls her Mel.

“I am pleased to write this reference for Melanie Apolinar Sotelo, who has worked as our family’s domestic helper since April 2024. Over this time she has become a valued and trusted part of our household, and I recommend her warmly.”

“Mel is a lovely-natured person who is kind and respectful to every member of our family. She is diligent in keeping our home clean and well kept, carrying out a thorough clean three times a week.”

“During her time with us, Mel has learned to cook for our family. She follows recipes carefully and with good results, and she also takes the initiative to find new recipes on YouTube for us to try, which we have very much appreciated.”

“Mel also babysits our daughter whenever needed. She cares for our daughter deeply, and we have always felt complete peace of mind leaving her in Mel’s care.”

“Mel is leaving our employment only because our family’s circumstances have changed, and we have mutually agreed to part ways. Her departure is in no way a reflection of her performance or character.”

“I have no hesitation in recommending Mel to any family looking for a caring, reliable and hardworking helper. If you would like to discuss her further, please feel free to contact me directly.”

That last line is the one we would take him up on. A family seriously considering her can speak to him directly, and we will arrange it.

Her record

First in Singapore in 2011, and two and a half years in the household she is in now.

April 2024 to now — two and a half years. An Australian-Indian family in a condominium, with their daughter, now nine: the cooking, the groceries, the laundry, the school run and the house. This is the household that wrote the letter on this page.

March 2023 to April 2024. A Dutch-Brazilian family of four with two small children — a baby of six months and a child of three — and their pets. Her record for that placement carries the care of an infant, a toddler and pets alongside the cooking and the house.

2017. An Australian couple and their newborn daughter, whom she took from birth and looked after for five months, with the cooking and the house besides. She took a cooking course at Expat Kitchen and a first-aid course in the same year.

June 2011 to December 2014 — three and a half years. A British couple in a landed house, with two cats and three dogs. All the cooking, the marketing, the laundry and the housekeeping.

Before Singapore. Three years with a family of four in Manila, with children of one and seven, and five years before that as a cashier at a pension house in Baguio City.

Where she’d suit next. An expatriate family — that is every household she has worked for here, and where she is most at home. Children of any age, including a baby, and pets are no trouble. She cooks Western and Asian food and will work from a recipe, which suits a family with a kitchen of its own.

Ishy’s letter, his details for a reference call, and her full employment record are available to interested families, with her consent.
