Her record in Singapore

Since August 2016 — five households, with two spells home in between.

August 2016 to August 2018. Two private flats, each a household of three. The second was an Indian family, and she cooked Indian daily for the whole of it. She went home to Indonesia afterwards and was there until the end of 2020.

December 2020 to October 2021. A landed property with a household of six.

October 2021 to December 2023 — two years and two months. A Malay family of three in a three-room flat: the cooking, the household, a toddler and a child.

May 2024 to March 2026. A Chinese family of five in a three-room flat — the cooking, the household, a toddler, a child and the family’s pets. She went back to Indonesia at the end of it and returned to Singapore two months later.

May 2026 to now. A household of four in a five-room flat, with a child of three and a baby of about eighteen months — the cleaning, and the cooking for the children. Her employer’s mother is moving in to take over the children, so the family no longer needs a helper, and they will give her a reference.

The kitchen is the thing. Fifteen years in the Middle East gave her Arabic food; each Singapore household since has added its own — Indian with one family, Chinese with another, and Korean, Thai, Japanese, Western and Indonesian with a third. In her last two jobs she has cooked only for the children, so a family who want the full range back would be getting something she has not used in a while and is keen to.

Where she’d suit next. A family with young children, and especially one whose kitchen is not the usual Singapore mix — Middle Eastern, Indian, Korean, Japanese. She is used to a baby and a toddler in the same house, and she is fine with pets.

Her employment record, her full work history and a reference call with her current employer are available to interested families, with her consent.
