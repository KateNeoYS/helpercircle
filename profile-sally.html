Her employer’s recommendation

In his words, and ours.

Sally has been with her present family since August 2025, and her employer has written and signed a reference for her rather than leaving her to explain herself. His letter sets out what she did: the school runs, the sports practices and the play dates; the family’s meals; the cleaning, the laundry and the upkeep of the flat; and their son in the evenings when they were out.

His summary of her is short and worth reading twice. “Sally is reliable, trustworthy, and very caring. She was punctual, flexible, and completed every aspect of her job with the highest level of professionalism.”

Why she is available: the family are relocating to Vietnam. It is the second time this has happened to her. Before them she spent five and a half years, February 2020 to August 2025, with a household of five — three children, the youngest three and the eldest eight when she left — and that family returned to France for the mother’s work. Neither ending was about her. Both families would have kept her.

Which is why she has two referees. Her employer of five and a half years, now in France, has offered to speak to interested families directly. It is rare to be able to check a helper with two separate households, and rarer still when one of them kept her for more than five years.

The baking. Sally bakes, seriously and often — bread, focaccia, pizza she makes from the dough up, cinnamon rolls, cookies, brownies, muffins, flan, whole tables of cakes at Christmas. The photographs above are hers. For a family who like a house that smells of something in the oven, this is not a small thing.

Where she’d suit next: a family with children of two and up. She has done the toddler years and the school years, the pick-ups and the activities, and she cooks for a household properly. She has asked for children of two and above — babies are not what she is looking for.

Both her current and her previous employer have offered to answer questions from interested families directly.

From her kitchen

Photographs Sally took of her own baking and cooking. Most helpers send one or two pictures of dinner; she sent bread, pizza dough, cinnamon rolls and a Christmas table.
