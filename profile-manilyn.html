Why she is available

Her record

In Singapore since February 2021, with three families.

Before Singapore. Four and a half years working for a Chinese family in Manila, and two years as a cashier in a grocery shop in her home town. She raised her own two sons before any of it; they are teenagers now.

February 2021 to August 2023 — two and a half years. One household in a three-room flat, which her employment record shows as two registrations back to back at the same address type. She took their child as an infant — the first registration is for infant care — and from eighteen months walked him to and from school until he was four. That family moved back to Vietnam.

August 2023 to September 2025 — two years. A household of four, and the placement she talks about most. Their boy is autistic and non-verbal, and she had him from eighteen months to four: the feeding, the routine, the patience that work takes. It is worth asking her about it properly, because it is not something most helpers have done. That family returned to the Philippines.

September 2025 to now. An Australian family and their child of seven, with a dog, the cooking, the house and the car washed weekly. This is where the Western cooking in the photographs below comes from. They are moving to Thailand, which is why she is available.

Where she’d suit next. A family with children at almost any age — she has taken one from infancy, two from eighteen months, and has a seven-year-old now. A household with a child who needs more patience than most would find her unusually well prepared. She cooks Chinese and Western, is comfortable with dogs, and has run a home alongside the children throughout.

Christie’s letter and her full employment record are available to interested families, with her consent.
