She arrived to a newborn and a five-year-old. They are four and nine now, which means she has done the whole of one boy’s early childhood and four years of the other’s school life, alongside the cooking and the housework.

She is clear about two things for her next home, and we would rather publish them than have a family discover them at interview. She would like children of three and older — she has done the newborn years and would rather not start there again. And she is not comfortable with cats or dogs.
