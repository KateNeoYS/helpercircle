What her days have looked like

Elizabeth cooked for the household every day. Breakfast before school was simple and quick — a boiled egg, toast and Milo. Dinners were home-style Chinese food: ginger chicken, pork rib soup, stir-fried vegetables. She is happy to learn Western dishes, or to cook to a family’s own recipes and instructions.

She also looked after the boys on her own when their mother was away or the family was on holiday, and does the marketing herself when her Ma’am is busy.

There were no pets in the household, but Elizabeth is comfortable around animals — her family in the Philippines kept a dog — and is happy to look after a pet as part of the job, though it would be new to her in a working home.

Family she may suit

Elizabeth’s sixteen years were spent running one home steadily: cooking every day, keeping the household going, and being trusted with the boys on her own when their mother was away.

  • A couple or an adult household who want a home run steadily and without supervision.
  • A family who values someone staying for the long term, rather than a specialist for a short season.
  • English-speaking households. Elizabeth speaks English and Tagalog, and no Chinese or dialect.
  • Her ground is cooking and running a household. She has not done personal or elderly care, and would be a stretch for a family hiring mainly to look after an older parent.
