Her record

Two families in ten years, and she is still with the second.

2016 to 2020 — four years. One family in Saudi Arabia: the house, the cooking, the laundry and the ironing, and their children. This period sits outside Singapore, so it does not appear on her Singapore employment record.

May 2023 to now — three years and five months. A household of four in a landed home in Singapore, and her only employer here. She has had the same child throughout — five years old when she arrived, nine now: taking her to school and fetching her, the activities, the reading and the playing, alongside the cleaning and the ironing. Her employment record carries this as one unbroken registration, which is what a renewed contract looks like.

She has not been the only helper in that house. There are two, and the work is divided — which is why the cooking has only sometimes been hers, and why a family where she would be on her own should talk the day through with her first. She cooks well, as the photographs show; she has not had to carry a whole household alone.

Why she is leaving. The child she has looked after for three and a half years can manage by herself now. The family are giving her a release letter — which is a document, not a recommendation, and we would rather be clear about the difference.

Where she’d suit next. A family with a child of three or older — she asks for that herself, and a school-age child is what she has done. She is good at the shape of a school day: the mornings, the fetching, the activities, the sitting down to read. She has not worked with pets or elderly parents.

Her full employment history is available to interested families, with her consent.
